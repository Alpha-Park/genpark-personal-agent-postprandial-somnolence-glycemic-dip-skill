# Personal Agent Postprandial Somnolence Glycemic Dip Skill

Designed for **OpenClaw**, **Hermes**, and autonomous personal AI assistants to evaluate post-meal cognitive performance degradation, quantify the Postprandial Somnolence Index (PSI: 0-100), model lateral hypothalamic orexin/hypocretin silencing and mesenteric parasympathetic blood diversion, and schedule targeted glucose disposal walking protocols.

## Agent In-Context Capabilities

- **Glycemic Load & Caloric Density Modeling**: Computes rapid carbohydrate absorption vectors and reactive insulinogenic hypoglycemia risks.
- **Orexinergic Neuron Suppression Tracking**: Models the physiological silencing of wakefulness-promoting orexin neurons induced by elevated extracellular glucose and large neutral amino acids.
- **GLUT4 Ambulation Scheduling**: Prescribes non-insulin dependent skeletal muscle contraction protocols at the precise T+20 minute postprandial inflection point.

## Tools Summary

1. `evaluate_meal_somnolence_risk`: Returns meal Glycemic Load, PSI score, orexin suppression percentage, and onset latency.
2. `prescribe_postprandial_countermeasure`: Schedules exact clock time and duration for post-meal ambulation and posture guidance.
